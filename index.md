# Privacy Policy — Toyguard Rush: Robot Siege
Last updated: 2026-10-12

## English

Toyguard Rush: Robot Siege ("the game") is developed by Five Month Studio.

**Data the game stores.** The game saves your current stage, language, and sound settings on your device only. It has no accounts, no cloud saves, and no analytics. The developer does not receive this data.

**Advertising.** The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction data to serve, measure, and personalize ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Consent.** Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK, and some US states), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy > Privacy options.

**Children.** The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

**Deleting data.** Uninstalling the game removes all locally saved data. You can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/Lin060105/toyguard-rush-privacy/issues

## 繁體中文

《玩具守衛：機器人圍城》（以下稱「本遊戲」）由 Five Month Studio 開發。

**本遊戲儲存的資料。** 本遊戲只在你的裝置上儲存目前關卡、語言與音效設定。沒有帳號、雲端存檔或分析工具，開發者不會收到這些資料。

**廣告。** 本遊戲透過 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。Google 及其合作夥伴可能蒐集並使用裝置識別碼（例如廣告 ID）、IP 位址、大略位置與廣告互動資料，用於投放、評估與個人化廣告。詳見 Google 政策：https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**同意。** 在法律要求的地區（例如歐洲經濟區、英國與美國部分州），本遊戲會在請求廣告前以 Google 同意表單徵求你的同意。你可以隨時在「設定 > 隱私 > 隱私選項」變更選擇。

**兒童。** 本遊戲不以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，也不會刻意蒐集其個人資料。

**刪除資料。** 解除安裝本遊戲即刪除所有本機資料。你可以在 Android 設定中重設廣告 ID。

**聯絡方式。** 請至 https://github.com/Lin060105/toyguard-rush-privacy/issues 提出問題
